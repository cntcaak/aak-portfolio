⭐ Final Year Project

Design & Analysis of a 3D-Printed Piston (FDM)

Collaborated in a 2-person team to design a piston in SolidWorks, sized its internal diameter to a mating O-ring, and fabricated it via Fused Deposition Modeling (PLA). Applied a Taguchi Design of Experiments in Minitab across nozzle diameter, layer height, and travel speed to quantify their effect on print time — finding time decreased as each parameter increased. Performed thermal (convection) analysis and a mesh/tessellation study in ANSYS to evaluate the piston's thermal behavior and print-resolution trade-offs.
